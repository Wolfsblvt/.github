# Funding and professional arrangements

## Meaning

This guide explains optional support for Wolfsblvt projects and separates it from a product-specific paid offer,
professional agreement, or open-source promise. It does not activate a service, set a support commitment, or make every
Wolfsblvt Works product free or open source. GitHub does not inherit this guide as a community-health file.

## Optional support

[Support through Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/wolfsblvt). Voluntary support may fund maintenance, releases, domains,
infrastructure, artwork, and continued development. Contributions above direct running costs can fund further work.
A contribution is welcome, not required for ordinary use of a free product.

An account-level payment does not reactivate an archived repository or promise a response, a fix, private access,
priority, or roadmap influence. It does not change the license. Any sponsorship benefits must be explicitly described
in the actual active offer; no service benefit is created merely by inheriting `FUNDING.yml`.

## Project-specific recognition

Where offered, supporters may opt into recognition on the project they select. A company sponsorship may include an
approved logo, link, or concise verified statement about its use of that project. Such placements are identified as
sponsorship, not certification or ownership. They do not automatically purchase advertising across other projects,
and private supporters remain private.

## Paid offerings and complete-open-source products

A project may offer paid store distribution, operated hosting or connectivity, or other genuine convenience alongside
its free routes. Each offer states what payment includes. A continuing service may require a recurring plan even when
the application is available without charge. A one-time app purchase does not imply lifetime online service.

Some projects explicitly adopt a complete-open-source promise: the complete named product or edition remains available
through its public source and official free distribution while maintained, without deliberately removed features,
withheld fixes or documentation, or artificial friction created merely to sell relief. That promise applies only to
the project or edition that actually adopts it, not automatically to every repository using this funding destination.

Wolfsblvt Works may also make proprietary applications, commercial games, SaaS, or open-source components with
separately scoped paid services. Those products state their own boundaries rather than inheriting another project's
funding model. An open-source license can permit commercial use without promising free hosting or maintenance.

## Professional and commercial enquiries

Setup, deployment, integration, sponsored open-source development, and support arrangements may be available by
agreement. Alternative licensing may be discussed where the necessary rights exist; ordinary commercial permission
already granted by an open-source license does not need to be bought again.

Use the relevant project's published contact route for enquiries. Voluntary support and an enquiry do not create
availability, free consulting, delivery dates, response-time commitments, or a professional agreement.

## License and contribution boundaries

Each project's own license and contribution policy govern its code and accepted contributions. The shared
[contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md#licensing-and-commercial-use) explains ordinary commercial reuse without imposing
an account-wide CLA or granting additional relicensing rights.
